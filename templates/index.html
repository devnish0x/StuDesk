Frequently Asked Questions

What is StuDesk?

StuDesk is a campus complaint management platform. Students can report issues, track progress, and share feedback — all in one place.

How do I submit a complaint?

Log in with your student account, fill in the complaint form with a description of the issue, and submit. It will then be reviewed and directed to the right department.

How can I track my complaint?

Go to "My Complaints" after logging in. Each complaint shows its current status — Pending, In Progress, or Resolved.

Who handles submitted complaints?

The relevant department staff handle complaints, overseen by the platform admin. Only you, the assigned department staff, and the admin can see your complaint.

How can I submit feedback or suggestions?

After logging in, use the Feedback section to rate a resolved complaint, or use Suggestions to share ideas for improving campus services.

Ready to Report an Issue?

Log in to StuDesk and submit your campus concern. No follow-up chasing required.
