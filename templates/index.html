Questions students ask

Is my feedback anonymous?

Yes, if you tick the anonymous box. Admins will not see your name, but you can still find your own submissions.

What if the department is wrong?

Staff can move your complaint to the right department, so you do not need to submit it again.

Who can see my complaint?

Only you, the staff of the department it is assigned to, and the admin.
